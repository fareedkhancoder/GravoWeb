The story behind the app

Built around the
everyday shopping list.

Gravo is an Android shopping app that brings product discovery, cart management, checkout and order information into one convenient place.

What Gravo is about

Useful first. Simple by design.

Everyday shopping is made up of small decisions — what to buy, how much to add, which delivery option is available and where an order stands. Gravo is designed to make those steps feel more organised.

We are building the experience around clear product information, an easy-to-review cart, useful account tools and order updates that help customers know what is happening next.

  • Shopping essentials in one Android app
  • Location-aware availability and delivery options
  • Account, order history and tracking tools

What matters to us

Good service starts with clarity.

◎

Local relevance

Availability and delivery information should reflect the address selected by the customer.

✓

Clear communication

Order states and important account information should be easy to find and understand.

♡

Respect for customers

Privacy information and account deletion instructions should be accessible and understandable.
