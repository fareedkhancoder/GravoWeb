What may be deleted?
After verification, account profile information, saved delivery addresses, cart data and active account information may be deleted, subject to operational and legal requirements.
What may be retained?
Order invoices, transaction records and limited information needed for tax, accounting, legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes may be retained for the required period. Where appropriate, records may be anonymised or separated from your active account.
For questions, email gravo.shopping@gmail.com. For general privacy questions, see the Privacy Policy.
