Find answers about getting started, delivery, orders, support and your account.

What is Gravo?

Gravo is an Android shopping app where you can browse available products, add items to your cart, check out and view order information.

Where can I download Gravo?

You can download the Gravo Android app from Google Play using the download link on this website.

Does Gravo deliver to every location?

No. Delivery availability, products, delivery windows and charges depend on the service area. Select your address in the app to see the options available to you.

How do I place an order?

Choose your delivery address, browse available products, add items to your cart, review your order and continue through checkout using the options shown in the app.

How can I track my order?

Open the orders area in the Gravo app and select an order to view its latest available status and tracking information.

Which payment methods are supported?

Payment methods can vary. The options currently available for your order are displayed in the Gravo app during checkout.

How can I contact Gravo support?

Email khanfareed01131@gmail.com with a short description of your issue. Include relevant order information, but never send your password, UPI PIN or complete card details.

How do I request account deletion?

Visit the Account Deletion page on this website and follow the instructions. You will need to send the prepared request from your own email app so the team can verify it.

Where can I read how my data is used?

See the Privacy Policy page for details about information collected, its use, service providers, retention and your choices.
