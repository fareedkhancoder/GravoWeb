A simple shopping journey

From your list
to your doorstep.

Here is how to get started with Gravo and what to expect when you place an order.

Step one

Choose your address

Open Gravo and select or add the delivery address where you want to receive your order.

Step two

Browse and add items

Explore the products available for your address and add your choices to the cart.

Step three

Review and checkout

Check quantities, delivery details and the payment options presented in the app before placing the order.

Local availability

Your address helps show the right options.

Delivery areas, estimated windows, product availability and charges may vary by location and order. Gravo shows the applicable information in the app after you select an address and during checkout.

This website does not check live delivery serviceability. Please use the Gravo app to confirm whether delivery is available for your address.
