Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Gravo app (the "Application") for mobile devices, an e-commerce platform operated by Meherbaan (collectively, the "Service Provider").


Information Collection and Use

To facilitate shopping and delivery, the Application collects information when you download, register, and use it. This information includes, but is not limited to:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, and phone number (for authentication and order updates).
  • Delivery Data: Shipping addresses and PIN codes to determine delivery serviceability.
  • Transaction Data: Order history, cart contents, product variants selected, and billing details.
  • Payment Data: Payment processing is handled securely by Razorpay. The Application does not store complete UPI PINs, credit card numbers, or bank account passwords on its servers.
  • Device Data: Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, mobile operating system, and time spent on the Application.

Location Information

The Application may collect your device's location to provide location-based e-commerce features.

  • Geolocation Services: Used to verify delivery serviceability in operational areas and optimize local delivery routes.
  • Analytics and Improvements: Aggregated location data helps the Service Provider map demand and improve quick-commerce operations.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, pixels, local storage (such as SQLite/Room databases), and similar technologies to support offline functionality, maintain cart states, and manage user sessions.


Third Party Access & Integrations

The Service Provider utilizes trusted third-party services for backend infrastructure, authentication, and payment processing. These third parties have their own Privacy Policies regarding data handling:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions)
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Google Analytics
  • Razorpay (Payment Gateway)

The Service Provider may disclose your information to these providers strictly to facilitate app functionality, process orders, and complete transactions.

  • Access the personal data (including order history and addresses) held by the Service Provider.
  • Correct any inaccurate or outdated information in your profile.
  • Request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains personal data based on its necessity for e-commerce fulfillment and legal compliance:

  • Transaction Records: Order invoices and payment receipts are retained as required by Indian tax and commercial laws.

If you request account deletion, your profile will be removed, but transactional records may be kept in an anonymized or legally compliant format for auditing purposes.


Security

The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your information. This includes atomic database transactions to secure inventory locking, encrypted payment webhooks, and secure cloud infrastructure.
