Your information matters

Privacy, explained
in plain language.

This policy describes how Gravo handles information when you use our Android shopping application and related services.

At a glance: Gravo uses information to support sign-in, shopping, order fulfilment, delivery serviceability, payments and app improvement. You can contact us to ask about your data or request account deletion.

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Version 2.0

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Gravo mobile application (the “Application”), an e-commerce and shopping service provided by Meherbaan (“Gravo”, “we”, “us” or “our”). It also covers information you provide when contacting Gravo through the website or support email.

2. Information we may collect

The information collected depends on the features you use. It may include:

  • Account information: name, phone number and email address used for registration, authentication and account support.
  • Delivery information: saved delivery addresses, PIN codes and, when you choose to use a location feature, device location information used to assess delivery serviceability.
  • Shopping and transaction information: cart contents, products and variants selected, order history, invoices, billing information and order status.
  • Payment-related information: payment status, transaction references and related checkout information. Payment processing is handled by payment providers such as Razorpay. Gravo does not ask you to share your UPI PIN or banking password with us.
  • Device and usage information: device details, operating system, IP address, diagnostic information and app interaction data that may be collected by the Application or its service providers.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting customer support, requesting account changes or asking us to delete your data.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to operate and improve the service, including to:

  • create and secure your account and authenticate sign-in;
  • show products, maintain carts and process orders;
  • verify delivery serviceability and coordinate fulfilment;
  • process payments, refunds or payment-status checks through the applicable provider;
  • send order-related notifications and respond to support requests;
  • detect abuse, prevent fraud, troubleshoot technical problems and maintain service security;
  • understand aggregated usage and improve the Application; and
  • meet applicable legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

4. Location information

If you grant location permission or enter a delivery address, the Application may use that information to check whether delivery services are available for the selected area and to support local fulfilment. You can manage device-level location permission through your Android settings. Manually entered delivery addresses may still be used to fulfil orders and check serviceability.

5. Service providers and sharing

We may share information with service providers where necessary to deliver Application features and operate the service. The existing Gravo policy identifies the following integrations:

  • Firebase for services such as authentication, database, cloud functions and related infrastructure;
  • Google Play Services and related Google services;
  • Razorpay for payment processing; and
  • analytics, diagnostics, delivery and operational providers used to support the service.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or to investigate fraud and misuse.

6. Cookies, local storage and app technologies

The Application and its service providers may use local storage, databases on your device, identifiers, SDKs and similar technologies to maintain sessions, support cart and offline features, diagnose crashes and understand app usage. This website itself does not require you to create an account to read its public pages.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your active account, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal and accounting obligations. Account information and saved addresses may be retained while your account is active. Order invoices and transaction records may need to be retained for the period required by law even after an account deletion request.

Where information is no longer required, we will take steps to delete it or retain it in a form that is no longer linked to an identifiable individual, subject to legal and operational requirements.

8. Your choices and requests

You can update certain account information in the Application and manage device permissions in Android settings. You may also contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. Requests may require identity verification to protect your account.

To request deletion, use the Gravo Account & Data Deletion page. Deletion may not remove transaction records that must be retained for legal, tax, accounting or fraud-prevention purposes.

9. Children

The Application is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us using the details below.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No electronic service or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please protect your sign-in credentials and never share passwords, UPI PINs or banking credentials with anyone claiming to be Gravo support.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Application, our service providers or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this version was published. Please review this page periodically for updates.
